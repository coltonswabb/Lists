# Decree privacy policy

_Last updated 24 November 2026_

Decree is a to-do list app. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: your lists stay on your device and the developer never receives them.

## What Decree stores

Everything you write in Decree (your scrolls, their items, reminders, the Chronicle of finished items, and your settings) is stored on your device. Decree has no accounts and no server of its own, and it does not send your lists anywhere.

## What Decree does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
- It does not share or sell data to anyone.

## Features that touch other parts of your device

- **Reminders.** If you add a reminder to an item, Decree asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through a server.
- **Widgets.** If you add a Decree widget, the names of your items are shared with the widget on the same device so it can show them.
- **Backups.** Settings can save your scrolls to a file through the system share sheet, and restore from one. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

## App updates

Decree may check Expo's update service for a newer version of the app's own code. That request carries technical details about the app, such as its version and platform, and none of your lists or settings.

## Children

Decree does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
