# Decree support

Decree is a gesture-driven list app in which every list is a scroll.

## Getting help

Report a problem or ask a question at https://github.com/coltonswabb/lists/issues. Please include your device, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**How do I add an item?** Pull down inside a scroll and let go, then type. Keep pressing Return to add several in a row.

**How do I finish or remove an item?** Swipe it right to strike it done, or left to cast it out. A notice at the bottom restores the last thing you removed.

**How do I clear finished items?** At the end of a scroll, keep pulling up.

**How do I set a reminder?** End an item with a time, such as "@ 5pm" or "@ tomorrow 9am".

**How do I make sections?** End a row with a colon, such as "Produce:".

**How do I move my lists to a new phone?** In Settings, back up your library to a file, then restore from that file on the new phone.

**Where are my lists stored?** Only on your device. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
